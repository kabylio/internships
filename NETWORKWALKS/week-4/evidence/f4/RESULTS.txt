Finding F4 — Weak PDF Password Protection

Verification method:
- Extracted PDF password hashes from three locally available PDF copies.
- Performed offline dictionary-based recovery with John the Ripper.
- All three password hashes were recovered.
- Validated the recovered passwords using qpdf --check.

Observed:
- Password hashes tested: 3
- Password hashes recovered: 3
- Password hashes remaining: 0
- PDF encryption revision reported by qpdf: R=3
- Encryption key length: 128-bit

Conclusion:
All three tested local PDF copies were protected by weak passwords
that could be recovered through offline dictionary-based testing.

Limitations:
The tested local files have not been conclusively reconciled with
all PDF copies referenced in the original assessment report.
Plaintext passwords and raw extracted hashes are intentionally
excluded from this evidence record.
